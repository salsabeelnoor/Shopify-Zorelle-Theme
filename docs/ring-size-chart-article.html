The perfect ring should slide over your knuckle with a little resistance and sit comfortably without spinning. Use this guide to find your size at home in a few minutes, then match it to our chart below.

Before you measure

  • Measure at the end of the day, when your fingers are at their largest.
  • Avoid measuring when your hands are cold, as fingers shrink slightly.
  • Measure the exact finger you will wear the ring on. Your dominant hand is usually a little larger.
  • Measure two or three times to be sure.

Two easy ways to find your size

01

Measure a ring you already own

  1. Choose a ring that fits the finger you are buying for.
  2. Place it on a ruler and measure the inside diameter from edge to edge, in millimetres.
  3. Find that number in the Diameter column of the chart below.

02

Measure your finger

  1. Wrap a thin strip of paper or string around the base of your finger.
  2. Mark the point where the ends meet. Make sure it is snug but can still slide over your knuckle.
  3. Lay it flat and measure the length in millimetres. Find that number in the Circumference column.

Tips for the perfect fit

  • Between two sizes? Choose the larger one, especially if your knuckles are wider than the base of your finger.
  • Wide bands (6 mm or more) feel tighter. Go up half a size.
  • Stacking rings sit close together, so a snug fit keeps them from turning.
  • Buying a gift? Borrow a ring they wear on the same finger and measure it, or ask a friend or family member who knows their size.
